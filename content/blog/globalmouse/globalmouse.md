## Ranking & Review

### Relevancy: 
This meme is somewhat accurate to the lore of House M.D. In season 2 of the show, House captures a wild rat and names it Steve McQueen. Mouse bites are never actually used in the show to try and cure a patient, but it is considered a running gag in the House M.D. fandom. 

### AI Accuracy: 
Google Gemini's Nano Banana was used to develop this image. The agent accurately created House's face, but made the other characters into cartoon versions. In the show, Wilson does not have a beard. House's face is also not properly aligned on the giant mouse in the last panel. Gemini also repeated the top text on the bottom in the second and third panels. 

### Personal Thoughts: 
I found this meme to be very absurd, to which I will give it a rating of 7/10. Although the accuracy made me knock off a few points, I enjoyed how chaotic this meme turned out to be. 

### Overall Rating: 7/10
