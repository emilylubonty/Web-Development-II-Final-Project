## Ranking & Review

### Relevancy:
This meme is not very accurate to the lore of House M.D., but rather the running gag within the fandom. Mouse bites are not used in the show, but are jokingly considered as a treatment House would use in the show. Dr. Squeaky is not an actual character at all, since Steve McQueen is the name of House's pet mouse. 
### AI Accuracy:
Google Gemini was fairly accurate with generating the face of House himself. The beds in the background do not seem to be placed correctly, as one is facing towards House, while it seems another is perpendicular to the same bed. The mice are also cartoon like, which does not fit the realistic generation of House's image. There is also a black outline along half of the patient's folder in House's hand. The mouse bites are outlined with random red half circles and arrows. 

### Personal Thoughts:
I personally thought the mice were cute, but the meme did not impress me. It feels low effort, and I am unsure of where Dr. Squeaky came from. 

### Overall Rating: 4/10
